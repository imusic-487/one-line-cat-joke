## The joke

> My cat brought me a dead mouse as a gift, which is sweet until you realize he's been telling his friends I'm a terrible hunter.

One line, 127 characters. A gift with a twist: the mouse isn't a present,
it's a pity offering — and a bad review of my hunting skills.

## How it works

`main.py` holds the joke as a fixed string, checks it against the gate
(single line, 1–200 characters, not only whitespace), and writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
